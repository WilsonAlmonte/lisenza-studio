# Lisenza Studio — Landing Page Design Brief

## What This Is

An e-commerce landing page for Lisenza Studio, an artisanal home fragrance brand in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. Bilingual (Spanish primary, English secondary). Sales flow through a WhatsApp cart. This is a proposition demo for the business owner — include a demo banner at top.

## Mood Direction: "Maison Noire"

Elevated luxury. High contrast. Editorial sophistication. Think Diptyque meets Boy Smells meets Brooklyn Candle Studio. Poetic yet restrained — like a luxury fragrance house catalog.

### References

- diptyqueparis.com — editorial luxury fragrance
- boysmells.com — modern luxury candles, high contrast
- brooklyncandlestudio.com — botanical, refined typography
